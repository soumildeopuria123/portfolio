Arcade game · canvas

Little Wings — an illustrated Flappy Bird

A small arcade game drawn entirely in code: a coral bird, sculpted pipes and a layered landscape, playable in the browser in under a second.

  • Deterministic 120 Hz fixed-step physics on seeded courses, so a flight replays identically on any device or frame rate.

  • Every pixel and sound is code-native — canvas artwork and synthesized audio, with zero image or font requests.

Know me

I got into machine learning because I liked the moment a spreadsheet stops being a spreadsheet and starts telling a story.

Since then I've been teaching myself to build that story end to end: cleaning the data, engineering the features, comparing models honestly, and putting the result in front of someone who can use it. I care about evaluation metrics as much as interface details — a model nobody can inspect is a model nobody will trust.

Outside of code I've competed in Code Fusion, and I've collected more sports trophies than I have free shelf space for. That competitive habit is the same one that makes me refresh a leaderboard at 1 a.m.
